    Two agents, one house

    They share everything they know about the house. They work for different people.

    The concierge

    One for every guest, from booking to checkout.

    • Answers anything about the house, by voice or chat, at any hour.
    • Builds their days around the weather, the clock and who's in the party: kids, a dog, a late flight.
    • Books tours, boats and rentals from the local shelf.
    • Says it's an AI when asked, and hands anything urgent straight to you.

    Your chief of staff

    Works with you on every house, every stay.

    • Prepares each stay: the welcome message, a guide set up for that party, the weather and road closures they'll meet.
    • Turns every question the concierge couldn't answer into a drafted answer for you to approve.
    • Keeps the guide current: events, seasons, places that closed.
    • Sends a short note after each stay: what guests asked, what was booked, what to fix.
    • Changes nothing guests see without your OK.

    Video for the things words can't explain

    Pool heaters, fire pits, a finicky lock. We film the tricky parts during a walkthrough of each house, and the concierge shows the right clip the moment a guest asks.

    • Short and specific. One task, thirty seconds, filmed in the house itself.
    • In the answer, not in a library. “How do I turn on the pool heat?” gets the clip, not a link to a list of videos.
    • Kept up to date. Replace the heater, and the chief of staff asks for a new clip.
