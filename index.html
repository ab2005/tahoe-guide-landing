Arriving

From the car to the sofa

Check-in is any time after 4 PM. If the house is ready earlier, the hosts will message you.

1 · Drive

785 Tuolumne Drive

Open directions. This is bear country: take all food and drinks out of the car and lock it.

2 · Park

Driveway or garage

Up to 4 cars. Street parking isn't allowed at rentals here, and cars do get towed.

3 · Get in

The smart lock

Right above the front door handle: enter the code provided to you by Nicole. To lock, press the very top button.

Lock being finicky? Call Nicole right away: (530) 205-3434.

4 · Wi-Fi

In the guide

Open the guide from the link in your booking message — the network and password appear under House → What's the WiFi password. If they don't, just ask the hosts. The router is under the living room TV.

With your dog

Dogs are welcome here

The pine woods start right behind the house, and there's a dog-friendly beach you can walk to. A few house rules, and where you can — and can't — go together.

In the house

  • Off the furniture and beds, please — pet hair there means an extra cleaning charge.
  • Never alone in the house unless in a crate.
  • On a leash on walks in the neighborhood.
  • Pick up all dog poop around the house before check-out.

Where to go together

Rabbit Hole Beach dogs welcome

Officially Keys Beach — about 20 minutes on foot from the door. No fee, quiet, and the sunset is the reason to go.

Kiva Beach on a lead

The dog-friendly beach of the south shore. Kiva side only — not in or near the Taylor Creek marsh.

Walks leashed

The pine woods behind the house, the Camp Richardson path, Rainbow Trail at Taylor Creek, Van Sickle Bi-State Park.

Eating out patios

Base Camp Pizza (heated patio), Himmel Haus beer garden, The Idle Hour.

Leave the dog at home no dogs

Pope Beach, Baldwin Beach, Emerald Bay & Vikingsholm, the Heavenly Gondola, and the beach itself at Nevada Beach.

Ask the house anything

Don't look things up. Ask.

The guide has a concierge built in: an AI that knows this house and the lake. On the first screen, tap Talk and just speak — or type in Chat. It answers, suggests what fits the weather and the day, and puts it into your plan.

"Where can I walk the dog?" "How do I heat the sauna?" "Dinner nearby tonight?" "What should we do this afternoon?"

It's a computer and will say so if you ask. For anything urgent at the house, call Nicole: (530) 205-3434. Emergency: 911.

Before you leave

Check-out is 10 AM

Want to stay longer? Ask the hosts the night before — if there's no same-day turnaround, they'll gladly say yes.

  • Lock all windows and doors; lights and gas appliances off.
  • All trash into the bear box — the metal container right of the driveway. Not the blue or green bins.
  • Pick up after the dog around the house.
  • Furniture back where it was. No need to strip the beds or start laundry.
  • Triple-check for chargers and toys — returns cost $40 plus shipping.
  • Leaving early? A quick message lets the cleaners start sooner.
